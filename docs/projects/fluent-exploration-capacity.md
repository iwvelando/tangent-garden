# Fluent exploration and validated capacity

_Direction recorded November 25, 2026. Source audit based on main `5e950d0`, reconciled with the root-journey extension at `78ef8b2`._

The [garden stems](garden-stems.md) and [new mathematical gardens](physical-dynamics-fractals.md) broaden the art. This project makes that art easier to explore and asks how much more detail the existing mathematics can support. Two themes organize the work: a recognizable, direct interaction language across studies, and limits justified by numerical and device evidence.

This brief holds lasting intent. GitHub owns assignment scope, readiness, dependencies, experiments and completion. Follow [Project delivery](../contributing.md#project-delivery); put shipped mathematics, interfaces and usage in their permanent guides. The waves are themes, not a requirement to finish every earlier wave before starting them. Native issue blockers name the contracts actually consumed.

## Wave K — Consistent exploration

[Milestone](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/milestone/11) · Parent [UX · #570](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/570).

**The artwork is immediately available, and its controls are where the explorer expects them.** Give standalone workspaces a common language for entry/return, drawing and configuration placement, scroll ownership, playback and export. Short landscape screens must not require scrolling past a column to discover the adjacent controls. Establish the contract in Complex Loom and planar/spatial Draw a loop, then apply it to Hyperbolic Lace, Apollonian Garden, Curves that Smooth Themselves, all parameter atlases and guided journeys. Include alternate entry contexts, such as drawing a loop for a root journey, and grid, inspection and playback states.

Direct manipulation should be available in portrait 2D, 3D and 4D notebooks without the Adjust view gate. A deliberate drawing gesture belongs to the drawing; surrounding controls and page areas retain ordinary scrolling. The layout must keep navigation reachable without sacrificing useful artwork size. Drawing/editing and view manipulation may differ where their purpose requires it; differences must be understandable rather than accidental.

Finite engravings should invite exploration. Keep the member menu for direct selection and add quick stepping and discrete scrubbing with focused keyboard controls and usable touch targets. Hide an inactive engraving entry when the study has no supported family target; preserve a recovery path if an active study becomes incompatible. Probe and Finite engraving have independent hierarchy. Availability comes from the existing capability definitions rather than a list of preset names.

**A saved view becomes a journey through the art.** Extend Fly through key views to finite engravings, the 4D notebook, standalone workspaces, atlas inspections and guided journey stops. “A knot remembers” is a representative engraving. Reuse the existing editor, timing and camera interpolation, with explicit adapters for paired panes. Display cameras remain separate from 4D rotations, mathematical disk transforms, physical simulation time and atlas parameter paths. Define composition with reveal, moving windows and existing phrases without silently rewriting an authored preset. Gallery thumbnails and contact-sheet grids do not need their own camera editors.

**Watch a complex map draw its relationship.** Reveal the Loom's source and image together according to source parameters and interval provenance. A stretch or a gap in the image must remain visibly related to its source; independent screen-length reveals would lose that meaning. Preserve poles and discontinuities, and use one sampler for seeking, playback and actual paired exports.

## Wave L — Validated detail and headroom

[Milestone](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/milestone/12) · Parent [CAP · #571](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/571).

**Preserve the detail that made a study worth importing.** “Loops upon loops” and “Ripples hidden between samples” exceed the current source-outline budget when used in Draw a loop. A higher point ceiling alone is insufficient: source sampling, fitting resolution and the permitted frequency band are separate constraints. Compare an accurate bounded refit with preserving eligible analytic Fourier terms, explaining their different parameter semantics. Preserve the source's domain, orientation and identity. Imported terms must still satisfy each onward consumer's own mathematics, including lace motifs, shortening and root journeys.

**A limit needs a reason, and a default needs a different reason.** The [baseline inventory](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/571#issuecomment-7165975078) groups the garden's sampling, refinement, solvers, generative depth, family/atlas composition, retention, navigation, parser/links, transport and output limits. Its [declaration](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/571#issuecomment-7165975646) and [refusal](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/571#issuecomment-7165976189) appendices retain source anchors. They are audit evidence, not benchmarks or proof that each literal value is an absolute maximum.

Classify each bound before changing it:

- Work and storage ceilings need measured preparation, responsiveness, cancellation and retention evidence, including failed work and multiplied settings.
- Numerical and method domains need independent accuracy, conditioning and regularity evidence. More samples do not establish a safe extension by themselves.
- Parser, decompression and transport defenses must remain bounded under malformed and adversarial inputs.
- Product choices and ordinary defaults should serve useful art without inheriting the largest admitted request. Preserve deliberate preset artwork and old-link behavior.
- Actual browser, canvas and encoder capabilities remain device-specific checks rather than promises made by a larger menu option.

Recover prior evidence, establish a current baseline and then run bounded candidate experiments on a real iPhone and the owner's desktop. Define abort limits before testing; measure the real worker-to-visible path, seeking, cancellation, playback and output. Report sample counts and methods. Measured heap, retained-buffer accounting and enforced caps are different evidence. Use independent geometric or numerical oracles and inspect real output files. Simulation and headless frame rates cannot establish phone responsiveness.

Each inventory group receives a decision: raise, retain, lower, replace an admission estimate, or pursue separate numerical research. Record the reason, ordinary and preset defaults, affected mirrors and accepted delivery. A retained ceiling can be a valid outcome; an enforcement assertion alone cannot justify it. Approved improvements need delivery consumers, and a research gate cannot silently remove the work it was meant to inform.
