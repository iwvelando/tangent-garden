# Garden stems: extending the mathematical art expansion

_Proposed November 4, 2026. Capability audit grounded in `main` at `d1d4bc0` and the merged outcomes of the original sixteen expansion subjects._

The [original expansion](expansion-roadmap.md) planted distinct mathematical subjects and useful ways to explore them. This brief grows those established ideas: take a familiar curve into the harmonic workshop, let a thread follow another surface, compare more possible worlds, or watch a branch journey wind around a different path. Each extension should produce an image or motion worth discovering, not merely enable another control.

This is a design horizon, not a completion ledger or a claim that the original features were unfinished. Current acceptance, readiness, decisions and delivery belong in the linked issues. Later slices require design against the code that exists when they are assigned. Shipped mathematics and interfaces belong in the permanent guides. The existing [project delivery procedure](../contributing.md#project-delivery) governs implementation and review.

## Principles and limits

- **Make the relationship visible.** Offer a memorable sparse construction as well as the rich image: one thread in a textile, one circle in a packing, one source point in a transformed flower. Motion should expose that relationship.
- **Reuse the user's ways of working.** Keep the established notebook/excursion, selection, preferences, camera, sharing and export experience. Preserve source studies and raw drafts on return. Wider mathematical support must not create parallel export panels or state models.
- **Expand validated domains deliberately.** Specify which studies are admitted, why the mathematics applies, how singularities and unresolved samples are represented, and how total work is bounded. Validate each additional family through returned geometry and independent invariants. Keep existing links and drawings unless a deliberate change is accepted.
- **Compose only through an actual contract.** A sampled path needs gaps, parameter provenance and accuracy; a surface route needs chart and boundary semantics; an animation needs deterministic absolute-time sampling and complete-scene closure. A renderer or a visually similar curve alone is not that contract.
- **Earn generality with examples.** A curated second tessellation, seed or path is more useful than a nominally universal selector without reliable behavior. Research gates may change the admitted implementation, but cannot silently erase a parent's required outcome. Explicitly optional ideas stay optional.

The project remains a browser-based mathematical art explorer. These extensions do not authorize a scene editor, server computation, arbitrary polytope importer, general PDE solver or unbounded animation/family work. Captures remain geometry-only under the existing policy; the explicit planar phrase-caption exception does not automatically extend to other notebooks.

## Delivery waves

The waves group effort and design maturity, not calendar dates or global barriers. A later-wave slice can proceed once its own design and actual prerequisites are satisfied. Conversely, completing the first child does not complete a feature's horizon. Use native issue relationships when a specified consumer genuinely needs another slice; sharing a theme does not create a blocker.

### Wave E — Open more of the garden

[Delivery milestone](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/milestone/5).

Start with extensions that fit existing interfaces. Five independent first slices provide useful results without waiting for a new shared framework. The parent horizons include further work to specify separately.

**[S01](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/338) — Guided journeys through the expanded garden.** **A rule that changes worlds.** A visitor follows a small sequence from a thread on a surface to a turning arrow, or from a 4D shadow to a section and a branch journey. Each stop offers one revealing experiment and that study's actual motion; the user's own work remains intact.

Start with two journeys through the existing surface and 4D stories. Then admit overlay workspaces with explicit snapshot/adoption contracts and richer experiments where a scalar cannot explain the construction.

**[S02](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/339) — Parameter atlases across more studies and dimensions.** **A page of possible worlds.** Compare lights that weave different caustics, nearby knots and surfaces, or neighboring Hopf bouquets, then enter the one that catches the eye without losing the source study.

Add point-light positions and De Jong coefficients to the planar atlas, then extend admitted planar generators and create spatial/4D comparison flows with their own quality and camera contracts. Grid-path animation and a clearly labeled preview contact sheet are later consumers.

**[S05](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/342) — Harmonic fitting from existing curves and retained outlines.** **Watch a familiar curve rediscovered by circles.** Bring a roulette or closed expression into the fitting preview, compare its approximation, then send the fitted curve through the usual constructions.

Import an existing closed base curve into the harmonic fitting excursion first. Bounded outline editing/refitting and a separately defined spatial harmonic fit can deepen the same experience; persistent reference data requires a size and schema decision.

**[S06](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/343) — A richer complex loom with honest source composition.** **A grid becomes a many-petaled flower; a strip becomes rings.** Extend the map vocabulary while preserving the correspondence that makes the loom understandable.

Begin with bounded integer powers, then consider a bounded exponential strip, conditioned Möbius controls and gap-preserving derived paths. Small map compositions need propagated domains, singularities and combined refinement budgets.

**[S11](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/348) — Perspective and more dual correspondences in four dimensions.** **Look into the crystal, then compare two different kinds of cell.** Perspective makes the 24-cell's nested relationships legible; a cube–cross-polytope pair teaches duality beyond a self-dual example.

Give the existing 24-cell a safe perspective shadow, then add a tesseract/16-cell dual correspondence and compare the two in a journey. Preserve the distinction between polar duality and dualizing a section.

### Wave F — Compose and connect

[Delivery milestone](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/milestone/6).

Build on existing objects and give their paths, time and constructions compatible meanings. These tracks need defined interfaces and independently deliverable slices before assignment.

**[S03](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/340) — Finite engravings for more mathematical families.** **A knot remembers every shape it passed through.** A finite stack of spatial curves or minimal-surface boundaries becomes an intricate print, with one member selected to explain its construction.

Extend finite line engravings to selected planar generators and spatial curves, then analytic-surface boundary/grid families. Keep member identity and selected constructions; stacking meshes or multiplying existing native families is not a substitute for a bounded composition.

**[S04](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/341) — Editable phrases and composed motion in 3D and 4D.** **Explain the construction in one short film.** A geodesic grows, its arrow travels, the surface recedes, and the camera reveals the result; a Hopf path and its bouquet answer each other on one timeline.

Let users arrange a bounded planar phrase and compose a few active scalar tracks. Extend that deterministic sampler to spatial and descriptor-owned 4D stories, including cameras and paired views, before connecting it to other family-producing features.

**[S09](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/346) — Surface travelers and constructions on more analytic patches.** **A thread keeps its intrinsic route while a staircase opens into a throat.** Use the associate family's shared metric to carry the same geodesic through its unfolding, then reveal curvature with validated constructions.

Use cylinder benchmarks and the associate family’s shared metric to extend geodesics, then transport and further regular analytic patches. Validate minimal-surface offsets/focal constructions separately; single-event optics needs its own contract. Principal-direction traces remain an optional, distinct subject.

**[S10](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/347) — Hopf bouquets over a richer vocabulary of sphere paths.** **A winding line on a sphere becomes a woven bouquet.** Follow a spherical Lissajous path or a geodesic polygon, and see why distinct base points produce linked fibers.

Add analytic sphere paths, then a bounded geodesic polygon or explicitly supported traveler route, with full-path and local fiber sampling. Marked fiber phase requires a gauge rule so chart changes do not masquerade as physical holonomy.

**[S12](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/349) — Topology theater with more events and honest candidate discovery.** **Find the fleeting moment in your own field.** Discover candidate changes, inspect a bracket at higher resolution, and make a slow film without confusing the mesh's report with a theorem.

Add more analytically known events and multi-event navigation before attempting bounded candidate discovery in user fields. Report mesh evidence and uncertainty honestly; planar contour events require their own definitions.

### Wave G — Deeper roots

[Delivery milestone](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/milestone/7).

Broaden the numerical families through bounded mathematical and visual prototypes. Begin each uncertain capability with a research assignment specifying the experiment, independent evidence and owner decision gate. A promising picture alone does not establish admissibility.

**[S07](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/344) — More Apollonian seeds and tangency-preserving views.** **Different seeds grow different rose windows.** Compare asymmetric and symmetric packings, then watch a tangency-preserving change of view expose a hidden region without breaking the circles.

Derive and verify a small alternate-seed catalog, then a constrained editable family. Tangency-preserving inversive views need circle-to-line handling and justified pruning near poles; the original integer seed’s arithmetic cannot simply be assumed.

**[S08](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/345) — Hyperbolic lace beyond one tessellation and motif.** **Pentagonal flowers and square stars recede toward infinity.** Let the viewer compare a small set of mathematically different hyperbolic gardens and their sparse construction.

Prototype a second tessellation and its dual with verified addressing and adjacency, then add a curated motif vocabulary. Any later editor or idle-view regeneration must retain containment, deterministic finite coverage and frozen movie geometry.

**[S13](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/350) — Curve shortening from users’ own closed loops.** **Your leaf remembers a circle.** Bring a bounded closed outline into the established shrinking-contour study and watch its own features disappear while preserving honest physical time.

Establish a conservative admission and refinement contract for user-supplied closed loops before exposing import. The solver already supports nonconvex authored flowers; the new work is reliably retaining and evolving the user’s own features.

**[S14](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/351) — Branch journeys with winding paths and higher roots.** **A loop can circle a branch point—or miss it.** Shift and reshape the source route, compare its winding, and watch three or more branches exchange before the whole journey closes.

Compare translated circles that enclose or miss the branch point, then higher roots and their winding permutations. General parametric paths require controlled lifting; arbitrary polynomial equations or surface nets are separate research horizons, not a generic field to expose now.

**[S15](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/352) — Nodal gardens on circular domains and beyond the square.** **A round window fills with radial lace.** Follow diameters and nodal rings of a circular membrane, then rotate and mix an admitted pair while keeping the mathematical interpretation clear.

Prototype controlled disk eigenmodes and their Bessel zeros, then curate radial lace with honest domain boundaries and angular combinations. Rectangular aspect ratios offer another bounded extension; stationary modes, artistic sweeps and physical oscillation must remain distinguishable.

## Assignment map and maintenance

The five first-slice assignments are [S01.1](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/353), [S02.1](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/354), [S05.1](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/355), [S06.1](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/356) and [S11.1](https://github.com/iwvelando/tangent-garden/issues/357). They consume capabilities already shipped at the inspected baseline and do not depend on one another. This names the intended starting slices; consult their live readiness and prerequisites before assignment.

Subsequent issue bodies must name the exact capability they consume and expose. In particular, atlas paths may consume phrase tracks, Hopf routes may consume a surface-path interface, and shortening may consume harmonic outlines; none requires the entirety of the other feature parent. Split and link those deliveries when their contracts are known. Avoid a universal intermediate representation designed ahead of its first consumer.

Keep the wave milestones and issues as the delivery record, including scope changes, research decisions and follow-ups. Do not append completed/deferred/open narratives here. The brief changes when direction changes; usage, mathematics, architecture and notebook guides change when behavior ships. Existing agent guidance and owner prompt skeletons already provide the implementation, exploration and reviewer handoff procedure.
